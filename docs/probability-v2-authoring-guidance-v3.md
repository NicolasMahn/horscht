# Development-guided authoring v3

This additive guidance applies only to new assignments explicitly marked `dev-guidance-v3`. Preserve every previous plan, prompt, raw output, skip and frozen training snapshot. Read the v2 guidance and obey the exact hashed assignment plan. The current turn covers at most eight assigned IDs, then persist and FINAL. Read compact pins from that plan; never trust manually copied pins. Use `dataset_factory.authoring_writer` to validate complete envelopes before durable append. Sparse drafts are not full rows and full-row validators do not accept them. Fix a draft before append; appended rows are immutable.

The first adoption is bounded to 4,096 fresh original assignments. The seeded planning mix is 70% choices 2–4, 20% 5–16, 8% 17–64 and 2% 65–265, conditional on documented task compatibility. Larger option sets must enumerate a meaningful complete inventory, transition, taxonomy, evidence set or constrained candidate collection. Every option must matter to the task and align with stable choice IDs and evidence/targets. Never pad with arbitrary alternatives, silently reduce counts or truncate choices. If a coherent fluent assignment cannot be authored, preserve an explicit scoped skip and explain the implementation limitation without claiming a proven contradiction.

Within compatible choices, modestly emphasize policy exceptions, graph/kinship, tradeoff optimization and procedure recovery. Retain broad operation and domain coverage. Domain facts must change the reasoning or target, rather than decorate a counting frame. Vary reasoning structure, conditional mechanisms, target shapes, answer positions, evidence and wording. Author independent new scenarios; do not copy internal development wording. Joint seeded choice shuffles preserve target/evidence/ID alignment. A linked counterfactual, translation or surface variant must record its parent/group and count separately from originals.

Preserve the global source targets: 300,000 exact-derived, 300,000 explicit stochastic, 100,000 empirical frequency, 100,000 teacher estimates and 200,000 hard labels. This keeps 80% soft supervision. Exact and stochastic evidence must fully specify the mechanism and rational probability derivation. Empirical evidence describes explicitly synthetic observations and declared counts, never an invented real experiment. Teacher estimates remain unverified subjective targets with actual model/provenance. Known outcome distributions are answerable even when realized outcomes are unknown. Answerable IDK has zero target mass; genuinely missing necessary information maps to task-grounded IDK. Cover both with independent scenarios across families, rather than forced near-duplicate pairs.

Before append, self-check that context, question, complete options, explanation, target and evidence agree; recompute numerical mechanisms and explain which facts decide each alternative. Check language fluency in the assigned output language. Choose general nonpersonalized subjects. Currently represented confident languages remain the main share. Small Japanese and Swahili shares use checkable reasoning targets; sparse DEV slices are not evidence of bad source text. Do not claim independent review or calibrated confidence. Model disagreement alone never proves an authored target invalid.

Issue ledgers use full canonical IDs and explicit scope_ids. Mark known wrong targets, option/evidence alignment, false IDK, missing decisive context, source provenance, or wrong-language defects as exclude_pending_review. Generic absence of independent semantic/language review and approximate word/sentence length shortfalls are review_recommended, unless an actual contract defect is identified. Preserve every raw row and old skip. A correction requires a narrow hash-bound receipt; never silently erase a flag or revive skipped IDs. Self-checks and deterministic mathematics are useful checks, not semantic gold or an originality guarantee.

Evidence is the paired 512-question internal DEV evaluation after both frozen matched arms completed. DEV was also used for checkpoint selection, so it is development evidence, not an untouched generalization claim. Teacher/review targets remain unverified, language and large-cardinality slices are small, and no 265-choice DEV question was available. Held source-test and external benchmark outcomes must never enter authoring decisions or prompts. After the bounded adoption, examine aggregate authoring integrity, skips, known defects, source/operation/language/cardinality coverage and unique-original accounting before choosing another profile. No bulk second-LLM review is authorized.
