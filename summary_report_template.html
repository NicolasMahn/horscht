What these scores mean

Familiar tasks are new questions from the four training task families. New tasks come from four families absent from our training set. Neither probe set updates model weights. We train only a classification head; the pretrained backbone stays frozen.

The pick maximizes mean familiar-task accuracy, with lower mean loss breaking a tie. All ten runs must pass the training target and saved-head checks. New-task scores are excluded from selection. A displayed score for an ineligible model is an observation, not a ranked contender.

Ten seeds vary head initialization, not the questions. These results do not establish performance on a broad population of tasks. The familiar probes are a development set because we use them to select models.
